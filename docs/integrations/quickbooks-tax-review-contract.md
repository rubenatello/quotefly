# QuickBooks tax review contract

The internal tax review contract and preparatory operation ledger are prerequisites for taxable invoice support. They are not connected to a route, provider request, or feature flag. The existing invoice publisher still blocks positive QuoteFly tax and explicitly publishes non-taxable lines.

`src/services/quickbooks-tax-review-contract.ts` validates a deliberately supplied snapshot of the invoice, customer, mappings, provider facts, and distinct origin/destination addresses. The initial address scope is the 50 US states and DC, with USD amounts. Territories, military addresses, exemptions, and unknown customer tax status need separate provider evidence before support is expanded.

The contract binds tenant, quote, invoice/version, customer, connection/realm/generation/environment, transaction date, mapping review versions/timestamps, customer and item provider versions, item tax-classification fingerprints, preference/company fingerprints, addresses, ordered lines, explicit TAXABLE/NON_TAXABLE intent, and exact invoice totals. A caller must obtain these facts under live tenant and manager authorization. Caller-provided fingerprints do not prove trusted origin or freshness.

Money enters as bounded decimal strings matching the existing Decimal(10,2) fields. Validation uses integer cents, including non-negative half-up rounding of quantity times unit price to the line amount. Inconsistent lines are rejected instead of silently changing their quantity or price. This local rounding rule still requires real QuickBooks parity evidence. The provider payload uses bounded JSON numbers only after validation.

The preparatory Estimate payload contains explicit customer/item references, USD, transaction date, ShipFromAddr, ShipAddr, and TAX/NON line codes. It omits manually supplied tax totals, rates, agencies, and internal costs. QuickBooks must calculate the actual tax. Intuit documents transaction address behavior and automatic tax requirements in its [Invoice API reference](https://developer.intuit.com/app/developer/qbo/docs/api/accounting/most-commonly-used/invoice).

Canonical source/payload hashes and an HMAC bind the complete review. Returned data is deeply frozen. Source JSON, payloads, hashes, and bindings are internal business data and must not be logged as content-free operational evidence. Only the separate assessment and fixed failure codes are suitable for that purpose. Every assessment retains `taxCalculationProven: false`, `estimateInvoiceParityProven: false`, and `publishingAuthorized: false`.

`src/services/quickbooks-tax-estimate-ledger.ts` persists signed reviews under live owner/admin authorization. The review key is derived from the current token-encryption key with a distinct HKDF purpose; the previous configured key supports an in-flight review during rotation. The ledger records immutable source/payload snapshots, separate stable Estimate and Invoice request IDs, and a single active revision per tenant/invoice. Composite foreign keys, forced tenant RLS, column-specific runtime grants, and database triggers protect identity and retained evidence.

Persistence and claim recheck the connection generation, realm binding, setup confirmation, invoice/version/lines, source lifecycle, and reviewed mappings. Only a draft invoice with pending payment, no amount paid, and its full total outstanding is eligible. The exact customer, job, quote, and invoice lifecycle rows remain locked through validation and persistence. Provider observations must be no older than five minutes, with at most five seconds of future clock skew. The signer is an internal trusted-reader boundary: no public route may sign client-supplied provider facts. Observation timestamps and fingerprints alone do not establish actual provider provenance.

The tax ledger and existing direct invoice publisher share a tenant-first, per-invoice lock. Any existing direct invoice operation or retained legacy provider Invoice ID blocks the tax path, including archived or soft-deleted evidence. Conversely, an active tax review or any attempted tax operation blocks direct invoice publishing. Superseded, never-attempted reviews release that block. This interlock prevents either workflow from ignoring the other's durable accounting evidence.

A claim lasts two minutes and records a separate, write-once hash of the original attempt token. Expiry moves the operation to reconciliation-required; it never authorizes another create. Only unattempted reviews can be superseded through this service. A matching late response may retain its provider Estimate ID after lease expiry, disconnection, or supersession, without authorizing a new provider request. Conflicting identities are rejected. No helper currently transitions an operation to canonical or changes financial totals.

There is intentionally no provider adapter or linked Invoice builder yet. The next implementation needs trusted provider observations, dispatch-time authorization and source revalidation, immediate persistence of returned provider IDs, and reconciliation of ambiguous writes. Before publishing, canonical Estimate and full linked Invoice reads must establish exact customer, address, line, subtotal, tax, total, and link parity. A positive-tax sandbox fixture must produce positive provider-calculated tax; an exempt or zero-tax response cannot substitute for it.

The existing source model cannot yet assemble an authoritative tax review: `Job.serviceAddressSnapshot` is free-form; the branding address has no country or confirmed sale/ship-from meaning; and Invoice has no explicit tax transaction-date field. Add structured, versioned, manager-confirmed origin/destination and a transaction-date policy before connecting the signer to an application workflow. Do not infer jurisdiction from free-form text or silently substitute invoice creation time.

The next bounded provider component can read strict Customer/Item tax projections and CompanyInfo/Preferences through the established credential wrapper, outside database transactions. Missing or ambiguous exemption/tax fields must fail closed. That wrapper's setup-confirmation fence remains mandatory; the OAuth-only staging connection cannot bypass it for a diagnostic. A later assembler must revalidate live authorization, connection generation, all source versions, and competing operations after the provider reads, then sign and persist internally. None of these prerequisites or mock tests substitute for actual sandbox tax behavior.

Focused contract checks run with `tsx --test tests/unit/quickbooks-tax-review-contract.test.ts` and are included in `npm run test:unit`. The schema and service suites in `tests/integration/quickbooks-tax-estimate-ledger-*.test.ts` require the migrated isolated test database and exercise the least-privileged runtime role. Passing these local checks does not establish provider compatibility, customer-facing tax support, or production release readiness.
